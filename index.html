Controls

Mouse, touch and keyboard

Selecting

Tap a Marine to select that Marine. Tap again to select the fire team, and again for the whole squad. The Squad and fire team buttons above the orders do the same. Keys: 1 2 3 fire teams, 0 squad, Esc cancel.

Moving

Pick Walk, Rush or Crawl, then tap the ground. Right-click also moves. Teams and the squad move in their formation. Rushing is fast but tiring and easy to spot. Crawling is slow but men can crawl while pinned.

Fire and maneuver

Suppress puts fire on an area, seen enemy or not. Bound sends the squad forward one fire team at a time while the others cover it (a fire team bounds in buddy pairs). Assault walks forward firing and throwing grenades. Tap a visible enemy to make the selection engage that target.

Reading the map

Blue circles are your Marines and red circles are enemy you can see. A dashed red diamond marks where an enemy was last seen. The orange ring is suppression: red means pinned, and a pinned man will not move except by crawling. The bar to the left of a symbol shows posture: tall for standing, short for kneeling, flat for prone. The numeral is the fire team.

Map key

The map is read like a military topographic map. White is open ground. Light green with small tufts is tall grass, which hides a man lying prone. Green is woods and hedgerows. Brown lines are contours, one meter apart, with a heavier, numbered index contour every five meters; numbers with a dot are hilltop heights. Solid black blocks are buildings. A black line with ticks is a stone wall, which gives hard cover to a kneeling or prone man. The double line is a dirt road. Grid lines are 100 meters apart; point at the map to read the 8-digit grid reference.

Camera

Drag to pan. Use the wheel or pinch to zoom. Space pauses; you can give orders while paused.
